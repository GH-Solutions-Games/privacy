Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate pieces?

Turn on Rotate pieces before starting a puzzle. Pieces then start randomly rotated. Tap a piece to turn it 90° clockwise. A piece must be upright before it snaps into place.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Progress is saved automatically on your device, so you can leave a puzzle and continue later.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Tap Custom and choose a photo from your library or take a new one with the camera. Your photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.

How do I delete a puzzle?

Open My Puzzles and tap the × on the puzzle you want to remove.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Puzzluma works offline. An internet connection is only used to show ads.

I lost my progress after reinstalling

All data is stored only on your device, so uninstalling the app removes your progress.
