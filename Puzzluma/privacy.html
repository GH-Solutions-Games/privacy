Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Puzzluma app (“Application”) for mobile devices, created by GH Solutions (“Service Provider”) as a free, ad-supported service.

What the Application stores
The Application works offline. Your puzzles, progress and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to the Service Provider. The Application has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email address or other personal information.

Photos and camera
If you create a puzzle from your own photo, the photo is selected through your device’s photo picker or camera and processed entirely on your device. Photos are never uploaded or shared.

Advertising
The Application shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device’s IP address, device type, operating system and advertising-related identifiers. The Application requests that all ads are non-personalized, child-directed and limited to content suitable for all ages. Google’s handling of this data is described in its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

The Service Provider does not itself collect, store or sell any personal data.

Children
The Application is suitable for all ages, including children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and ads are configured as child-directed (non-personalized, all-ages content) in accordance with COPPA and GDPR. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, contact kenneth@gh-solutions.dk.

Data deletion
All app data is stored locally and is deleted when you uninstall the Application. You can also delete individual puzzles inside the app.

Changes
This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-10-29.
